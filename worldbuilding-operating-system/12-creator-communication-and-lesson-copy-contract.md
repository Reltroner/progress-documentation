# 12 — Creator Communication and Lesson-Copy Contract

**Status:** PROPOSED editorial specification, not ratified or released. **Date:** 2026-10-23.  
**Source hierarchy:** [binding Master Source of Truth](https://github.com/Reltroner/reltroner-studio/blob/main/content/principles/reltroner-studio-master-source-of-truth.md) → [subordinate proposed Communication Framework](https://github.com/Reltroner/reltroner-studio/blob/main/content/principles/reltroner-studio-communication-framework.md) → candidate course documentation.

## 1. A human problem before architecture

A creator may have fictional-world ideas but be unsure what follows. Treat this as a **hypothesis** until a learner expresses it. The intended value is an independently usable method for making one meaningful creative decision create the next question. This is creator education, not generic worldbuilding tips, an AI productivity product, a software course, a paid-mentorship funnel or an Asthortera canon initiation.

**Entry:** “I have an idea for a fictional world. What should happen next because of it?”  
**Mechanism:** a rule or constraint shapes an interaction; that interaction changes the world; the changed state generates an inherited question.  
**Evidence:** an inspectable, honestly labeled fictional example, followed by learner-owned practice.  
**Invitation:** optional, genuine next action, not mandatory enrollment or payment.

## 2. Communication depth L1–L5

| Level | Learner's question | Sample explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 — Human hook | Why should I care? | “What happens after the first interesting idea?” |
| L2 — Recognizable problem | Where am I getting stuck? | “Adding more facts may not show what changes.” |
| L3 — Value | What could help? | “Your last choice can create your next creative question.” |
| L4 — Method | How do I do it? | Use the six-stage exercise below |
| L5 — Architecture | What deep relationships should I verify? | World states, dependencies, continuity and canon |

Five **communication levels** are not five curriculum modules. The eight syllabus modules define learning coverage. Start at the shallowest adequate level and let the person choose deeper explanation. Brevity must not flatten the underlying cause/effect.

## 3. Message construction

For first contact, a course description or lesson introduction:

1. **Relevance:** a learner-recognizable, observed or explicitly hypothetical situation.
2. **Mechanism:** explain what changes, why and who/what experiences it.
3. **Evidence / honest limitation:** demonstrate an inspectable effect, but do not call that proof of general learning efficacy.
4. **Voluntary invitation:** one relevant next step that is actually available in the current channel.

**Short candidate copy:** “Have a world idea but not sure what happens next? Give it a meaningful limitation, follow one person's choice and see what new question emerges from the consequence.”

**Worked fictional example:** A city preserves memories in lanterns. Sharing requires consent. A real recording with a misleading explanation undermines civic trust. The city introduces review rules. Who can challenge the reviewers? One world rule created an interaction and a new question. **This is noncanonical illustration**, not a real technology, official Asthortera lore, or measured learner success.

## 4. Six-stage learner-owned practice

| Stage | Simple question | Minimum outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seed | What idea, person or place interests you? | One seed |
| Constraint | What limit or trade-off makes choices matter? | One rule |
| Interaction | Which people, values or systems meet? | One relationship |
| Consequence | What changes because of that interaction? | A causal effect |
| Changed state | What remains different? | S0 → S1 |
| New question | What becomes possible to ask only after S1? | One inherited next seed |

The [apex §47](https://github.com/Reltroner/reltroner-studio/blob/main/content/principles/reltroner-studio-master-source-of-truth.md) teaches a fuller **seed → system → interaction → consequence → changed state → new pressure → new question → new possibility → further interaction**. The six-step practice is a **learner-friendly translation**: the constraint helps define the system, and pressure/possibility can be unpacked later. Neither is a universally applicable law of fiction or the only possible creative workflow.

**Quality threshold:** a learner can point to which earlier change produced the new question. This is an **exercise check**, not a ratified pass mark or guarantee against creative stagnation.

## 5. Reusable lesson-writing pattern

Open every new lesson with a relatable scenario, show a small causal effect **before abstract terminology** when feasible, explain the concept, invite one learner-owned micro-task, state evidence and uncertainty, then offer optional depth.

**Module 05 example:** “A school uses memories from an archive. The archive changes its consent rules. Now the school must alter its lessons. Which two systems in your world change each other's decisions?” This demonstrates system interaction before demanding that a beginner understand institutional causal topology.

Avoid leading with terms such as *epistemic recursion, cross-domain system topology* and *multi-layer canonical dependency* unless the learner specifically needs that depth. Do not erase the real mechanism merely for brevity.

**Narrative disclosure:** Current events answer “what is happening?”, backstory answers “why is it like this?”, and a reference/wiki answers “how does it work?” These are optional ways of communicating a world, not three mandatory interfaces.

## 6. Evidence and claim ladder

| Evidence class | Responsible statement | Invalid leap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Candidate curriculum | “Eight modules and 89 lesson titles are proposed.” | “The complete course is live.” |
| Working fictional example | “This case illustrates a causal process.” | “Every learner will succeed.” |
| Learner explains the worked case | “A local example was understood in this observation.” | “Independent skill transfer is proven.” |
| Learner independently completes a second cycle | “Situated application was observed.” | “Permanent productivity is guaranteed.” |
| Verified production content | “This particular released lesson is accessible.” | “All candidate lessons are published.” |
| Future mentorship idea | “A possible offer pending separate authorization.” | “Book a guaranteed paid session now.” |

Do not conflate curiosity, comprehension, adoption, trust, endorsement, commercial intent, payment or retention. Free practice has intrinsic value without becoming an upsell.

## 7. Learner autonomy, intellectual property, privacy and AI

Paper or plain-text notes must remain valid learning media. Learners may revise or reject the method, use a small setting, choose their own genre, and keep their world private. Their own world is **not** Asthortera canon.

AI output, if voluntarily requested, is brainstorming input rather than automatic truth or canon. Sending notes to a third-party model is a distinct learner-initiated disclosure subject to that service's terms. Do not imply automatic uploads without inspecting deployed behavior. Public examples, testimonials or reuse of learner IP require explicit permission.

No guarantee of fame, sales, wellbeing, publication, permanent anti-stagnation or a universally superior fictional culture.

## 8. Editorial QA checklist

Apply the proposed COM-01–COM-15 principles to the actual artifact: accurate studio identity; meaningful audience context; one educational intent; appropriate L1–L5 explanation; causal mechanism; honest evidence; canon distinction; earned authority; optional legitimate next action; visual integrity when applicable; commercial truthfulness; privacy; scope protection; source reviewability; optional depth.

**Release gate:** documentation-only alignment is NOT acceptance of learner outcomes, live UI, compiled MDX, rights, infrastructure or paid offers. The actual content must be checked separately against existing LMS technical/publication contracts.
