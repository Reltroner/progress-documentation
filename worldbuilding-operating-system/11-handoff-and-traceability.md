## Stable conceptual invariants for the next editor

1. Method is **genre-agnostic**; don't transplant Asthortera's local canon constraints onto every learner.
2. Worldbuilding is **interconnected system design**, not lore volume.
3. Good creative constraints **generate consequences**.
4. Learners start small and select scale intentionally.
5. Character/community choices and daily life make abstract rules tangible.
6. Causality and state transitions explain why the world changes.
7. Previous meaningful output becomes structured input for the next creative cycle.
8. Draft freedom is preserved; canon status is explicit and scoped.
9. Reader-facing expression may be a scene, travelogue, reference or other format; writing a novel is optional.
10. Tools, AI and software are optional amplifiers.
11. Learner-owned agency is the success criterion, not dependence on a mentor or platform.
12. Proposal, approval, authorship, publication and measured learner outcomes are **separate evidence states**.
