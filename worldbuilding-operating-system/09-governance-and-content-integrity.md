# 09 — Curriculum Governance, Canon Integrity and Safety

**Status:** PROPOSED — 2026-10-14  
**Role:** Prevent a course proposal from silently turning into binding studio policy, published canon or fictitious learning evidence.

## Three distinct authorities

1. **Reltroner Studio apex** sets studio identity and binding creative principles. Source: [Master Source of Truth v2.3](https://github.com/Reltroner/reltroner-studio/blob/main/content/principles/reltroner-studio-master-source-of-truth.md).
2. **Course curriculum** defines how these principles are taught to beginners, once separately ratified; this folder is currently a proposal.
3. **Learner's world** is independently authored. Its rules, draft states and publication choices belong to its creator; a learning exercise does not alter studio canon.

Infrastructure or LMS engineering remains governed by its own frozen contracts and deployment evidence.

## Document statuses

- **PROPOSED:** unratified candidate for review.
- **RATIFIED:** explicitly approved as a binding curriculum decision by the designated owner, with date and version.
- **IN_AUTHORING:** lesson or assessment content being written, not yet published.
- **VALIDATED:** specified test or review produced inspectable evidence; scope must be named.
- **PUBLISHED:** verifiable release to learners in an identified environment.
- **SUPERSEDED:** old version preserved with replacement link.

Status must be scoped to a **specific artifact and evidence**, never automatically propagated across the entire LMS or business strategy.

## Learner-world claim statuses (suggested)

- Concept: raw idea with no structural commitment.
- Development draft: explored but revisable.
- Structured fact: accepted inside the learner's current private world model.
- Candidate for public canon: passed a planned continuity check.
- Published canon: publicly committed through the creator's chosen release process.
- Superseded draft: archived material with revision rationale.

Reltroner's stricter release doctrine applies to **Reltroner Studio publications**. In class, teach why versioning matters, but do not force every novice to publish or permanently freeze a world.

## Curriculum authoring quality gates (proposed)

Before marking a lesson ready:
- Objective and prerequisite are explicit.
- One key concept has a concrete, beginner-sized example.
- Claims are identified as world facts, fictional illustrations or methodological interpretation.
- A practice exercise produces inspectable evidence.
- Accessibility and plain-text options are available.
- Example doesn't assume all settings follow Asthortera's local laws.
- Questions can admit multiple coherent solutions.
- No unsupported outcome or market validation claims are made.

## Canon protection and epistemic integrity

Separate **facts about an authored fictional world**, **facts about the real world**, and **hypothetical design premises**. For science, institutions, economics, psychology or law, never label speculation as verified reality. Fictional distance is a legitimate teaching technique, not permission to misrepresent real-world evidence.

Avoid copying an Earth political narrative into a fictional world and calling renaming itself originality. Prefer causal translation to a creator's own coherent mechanisms. Preserve agency of local actors rather than resolving every conflict through a predetermined savior.

## Learner IP, privacy and AI

- Learner-generated worlds remain learner-owned unless a separately accepted license says otherwise.
- Private projects stay private by default; public examples need informed permission.
- If AI generates suggestions, require learner review of assumptions, continuity and authorship.
- Do not demand a camera or synchronous call for proof of creation.
- Avoid dependence on paid software, proprietary formats or network-only tools.
- No promise to prevent burnout or treat psychological distress.
- Do not market a course draft as an existing paid program or official credential.

## Proposed change-control record

Each substantive change records: **date; author; previous decision; proposed change; source authority; reason; affected modules/assignments; dependency effects; learner impact; risk; status; owner disposition**.

Minor typos or formatting can be fixed without pretending to have reratified the course. Preserve historic decisions and replace rather than erase contradicted conclusions.
