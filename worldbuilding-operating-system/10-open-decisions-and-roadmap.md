## Review sequence: smallest useful next steps

### Review 0 — Documentation completeness
- Verify all cross-links and lesson counts.
- Confirm this proposal respects master source and Asthortera boundaries.
- Capture source version and outstanding ambiguities.

### Review 1 — Redundancy and learning-load audit
- For each of 89 lessons, state one measurable outcome and a minimal example.
- Consolidate topics whose learning outcomes are truly the same.
- Identify terms requiring prior lessons and mark optional advanced content.
- Do not use module/lesson count as an achievement metric.

### Review 2 — Assignment coherence
- Pilot world premise → world rules → lived world → two-system map → state change → second cycle on **one miniature world**.
- Verify an absolute beginner can complete the arc without software, writing a novel or learning advanced science.
- Repair any assignment that produces disconnected lore rather than a dependency.

### Review 3 — Assessment calibration
- Test quiz discrimination with common misconceptions.
- Compare independent reviewers' interpretation of the capstone rubric.
- Decide resubmission and accessibility accommodations.
- Keep provisional scores separate from validated educational outcomes.

### Review 4 — Owner curriculum ratification
- Approve specific inventory version and frozen scope only after review evidence is inspected.
- Record exact files/commit and explicit owner decision.
- Set change procedure; no automatic claim of LMS production release.

### Review 5 — Authoring and delivery (requires separate authorization)
- Write lessons, examples, exercises, quiz items and answer rationales.
- Verify content quality, accessibility, copyright and consistency.
- Publish only through authorized LMS workflow and accepted technical contracts.
- Capture independent production evidence and distinguish it from Git commits.

## Completion vs evidence matrix

| State | What it proves | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Concept archived | Candidate design is recoverable | Ratification or learner comprehension |
| Curriculum ratified | Owner accepted a specific content scope | Lessons authored or LMS live |
| Content authored | Materials exist and were reviewed in stated scope | Deployment, learning effectiveness |
| LMS published | Specified assets available in named environment | Knowledge transfer or sustained use |
| Pilot evaluated | Observed learner outcome for a bounded sample | General effectiveness without further studies |

## Open questions for the owner

- Is 89 lessons too fragmented for first-time learners?
- Should communication/story expression remain one module or be integrated continuously?
- What must be demonstrable at the end of each lesson, not merely each module?
- Which content format is the minimum sufficient learner experience?
- What proportion of examples should be original micro-worlds vs carefully scoped Asthortera illustrations?
- What learner feedback constitutes evidence that the second-cycle method actually transfers?
- What language and delivery conventions best fit the intended learners?
