# 00 — Project Charter, Functions and Source Authority

**Status:** PROPOSED — 2026-10-14  
**Role:** Define what the course is, why it exists, what it does not promise, and which documents govern which concerns.

## 1. Problem statement

Beginning worldbuilders may have many concepts, characters, maps or story ideas yet struggle to connect them into a coherent fictional world. Additional lore does not necessarily resolve fragmented causality, contradictory foundations or the inability to generate the next meaningful creative step. This is a **learner need hypothesis**, not established market research.

## 2. Working course identity

**Title:** Worldbuilding Operating System  
**Subtitle:** Build Your Own Fictional World Through Systems Thinking  
**Positioning:** Beginner-first, genre-agnostic, cumulative, creator-owned, project-based.

An **operating system** here is a metaphor and practical method for managing creative inputs, world configuration, entities, interacting systems, state changes, expressive outputs, continuity and feedback. It is **not** a software operating system, required SaaS platform or tool certification.

### Intended creator-facing function

Enable a learner to **conceive, structure, connect, test, express, maintain and extend** a fictional world without permanent dependence on an instructor, AI, or unstructured inspiration.

### Main objective

By the end of the candidate course, learners should be able to design, organize, operate and independently expand their own fictional worlds using foundational systems thinking, causal reasoning, creative constraints and a repeatable worldbuilding workflow.

### Learner value hypothesis

The method may reduce fragmentation, improve causal coherence, increase creative continuity and make independent practice more satisfying. These effects are **to be tested**, not guarantees, claims to cure burnout, or proof of commercial demand.

## 3. Functional boundaries

| In scope | Not required / out of scope |
| --- | --- |
| Personal world premise and scale selection | A continent, empire, cosmology, planet or novel |
| Internal logic, constraints and justified exceptions | Asthortera's post-scarcity rules as universal truth |
| Characters, communities and lived daily life | One prescribed genre or political philosophy |
| Systems, causality, consequences and world states | Numerical science simulation or perfect real-world realism |
| A simple continuity register and repeatable loop | Git, Postman, code, AI, a wiki platform or paid tools |
| Learner-facing expression in chosen formats | Mandatory public release, monetization or fan metrics |
| One cumulative beginner capstone | Advanced mentorship, commercial launch, official certification |

## 4. Transferable principles vs setting-specific rules

**Transferable methodology:** question assumptions, define a premise, derive consequences, evaluate trade-offs, preserve internal consistency, distinguish observations from decisions, and let previous outputs become structured inputs.

**Asthortera-specific setting:** Reltronland, Depcutland, Astralis, Nytherion, the high-development baseline, unusual spatial mobility and particular national canon. These remain optional **illustrations only**.

The course must not teach that a scarcity-world, traditional medieval-fantasy world or small slice-of-life setting is invalid because it does not conform to Asthortera's macro-canon.

## 6. Success and failure modes

**Good evidence:** learner can explain how an event changed two interacting systems, detect a contradiction, revise a rule, and generate a genuinely dependent next possibility.

**Weak proxy:** many words, many countries, visual spectacle, number of AI prompts, or an attractive map without causal evidence.

**Failure modes to monitor:** premature complexity, formulaic worlds, one-size-fits-all settings, forced storytelling, hidden paywalls, AI replacing learner reasoning, and equating tutorial publication with actual learning.

## Creator-facing translation boundary (2026-10-23)

Introduce the course through the human problem **“What happens after your interesting world idea?”** and the causal mechanism **rule → choice → consequence → changed state → new question**, not a brand manifesto. Communicate one inspectable example and offer deeper details only on request. Creator education is the primary pathway; Asthortera's narrative experience is optional proof-of-work, not an obligatory learner canon. The [communication guide](https://github.com/Reltroner/reltroner-studio/blob/main/content/principles/reltroner-studio-communication-framework.md) is **proposed and subordinate** to the [apex](https://github.com/Reltroner/reltroner-studio/blob/main/content/principles/reltroner-studio-master-source-of-truth.md).

A course intention, worked fictional example, independently observed learner outcome, production release and actual purchase are **different evidence states**. Never guarantee productivity, cure stagnation, publishing success or income. AI, mentorship and public participation are optional.

## 7. Authority of this version

This document **proposes** a course, not a binding product decision. Ratification, production authorization and acceptance require separate recorded evidence.
