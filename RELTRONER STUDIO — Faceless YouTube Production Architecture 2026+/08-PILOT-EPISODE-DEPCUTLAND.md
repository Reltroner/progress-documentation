## 1. Editorial purpose

Demonstrate the contrast between a familiar cyberpunk aesthetic and a civilization whose advanced technology may be expressed through neoclassical architecture, collective memory, archival institutions, and epistemic discipline. The example is motivated by the Depcutland profile; it does not claim that every advanced civilization is non-cyberpunk or that visual style alone measures institutional sophistication.

**Central viewer question:** Must the visual appearance of an advanced society resemble neon cyberpunk?

**Value to the viewer:** another way to imagine cultural and institutional expressions of technological advancement.

**Do not:** begin with extensive GDP statistics, invent visual artifacts as canon, overstate Depcutland's development status, or imply the AI-generated images are literal screenshots of the fiction.

## 5. Proposed visual language

- **Palette:** restrained navy, graphite, soft pale stone, muted gold.
- **Architecture:** imposing neoclassical archive forms with sophisticated but unobtrusive technology.
- **Motion:** slow and deliberate rather than constant fast cuts; text-only scenes may use clean deterministic animation.
- **Type:** high contrast, minimal copy, large mobile-readable size.
- **Backgrounds:** studio-owned artwork first, authorized Flow generation only if available and useful, otherwise generated diagrams/still-image parallax.
- **Style warning:** do not treat every imaginary depiction as factual canon; label conceptual art in the internal asset manifest.
