## Executive snapshot

**Reltroner Studio sells intellectual value, not its technology stack.** Its apex value is **Creative Compound Machine** (helping creators develop interconnected, reusable creative systems) plus **Asthortera / The Abyss of Comfort** (advanced sci-fantasy civilizational simulation exploring familiar human anxieties of 2026+). Website, LMS, technology, publishing and future paid offers are delivery/access/distribution media.

Three capabilities can operate in synergy: **Systemic Structural Creative Design × Software Engineering × Self Improvement**. They are complementary capacities rather than three automatically separate businesses.

**Ratified phase sequence:** Phase 1 completes accepted Reltroner LMS full production DoD plus production-standard *front-end* reltroner.com landing UX and operational handoff; only then may founder-readiness assessment and explicit activation open Phase 2 community branding. Phase 2 shifts engineering to maintenance-first and verified business-needs changes, not unlimited new feature creation.

**Snapshot status:** Phase 1 has **not** been certified complete in these sources; Phase 2 is **not activated**. The latest reviewed LMS Phase 4B G03 source/CI milestone is not live/production acceptance. Never infer current status without revisiting [the active LMS ledger](../lms/engineering-end-to-end-progress-ledger.md).

## Status and source-of-truth discipline
- This archive is **not** a clone of the complete Master Constitution or a replacement for the entire LMS engineering ledger.
- Strategic ratification does not mean a feature exists or production is ready.
- Previously discussed ideas about additional Keycloak realms, browser sessions/BFF, guest LLM chatbot, pgvector, Hermes, LLM Wiki, external meeting/DM vendors and price tiers **remain candidates unless accepted by their own scoped authority**.
- The frozen LMS topology is **API Gateway + Learning + Mentorship + Knowledge + Assistant + Audit**. Public surfaces include learner, separate admin frontend and only Gateway as public LMS API.
- No speculative revenue, follower growth, learner-burnout improvement, publishing achievement or business traction is asserted.
- Unpublished The Abyss of Comfort Season 1–37 remains mutable while planning; public production-locked novel releases are a separate milestone.
- This is a **public repository**: do not add secrets, private learner records, privileged system logs or confidential personal data.
