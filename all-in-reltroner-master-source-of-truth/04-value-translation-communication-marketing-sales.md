# 04 — Reltroner Value Translation Framework

> Status: RATIFIED_STRATEGY; explains value, does not manufacture market evidence.

## The translation problem
Reltroner Studio may internally reason with hundreds of dependencies. Stakeholders should not need to receive the whole internal model to begin caring. **The studio does not sell complexity; it uses complexity to produce clarity, possibility and experience.**

## Five-resolution ladder

| Level | Message resolution | Appropriate context |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Human hook** — familiar tension or question | first contact |
| 2 | **Relevant problem** — what the person actually struggles with | discovery |
| 3 | **Value proposition** — what changes for this person | explanation |
| 4 | **Mechanism/methodology** — why/how the method works | interested creator, serious reader |
| 5 | **Internal architecture** — graphs, constraints, consistency, release contracts | deep collaborator or internal design |

Move up/down based on curiosity and comprehension. Do not assume short content is intrinsically shallow or that exposing jargon proves expertise.

## Creator-facing translation
~~~text
“I have characters/world ideas but keep losing momentum.”
→ diagnose isolated creative units and weak causal propagation
→ explain seed → constraint → consequence → new state
→ demonstrate with a bounded case study (Asthortera when relevant)
→ invite learner to apply to their own project
→ measure independent artifact and response, not just likes
~~~

Possible promise: “A method for making previous creative work generate future possibilities.” Not “guaranteed publishing success or burnout cure”.

## Audience-facing translation
~~~text
recognizable 2026+ anxiety
→ specific “what if?”
→ advanced Asthortera scenario
→ character experience + institutional reaction
→ emergent cross-domain consequences
→ reader reflection toward Earth
~~~

Readers need not be interested in the framework as a tool in order to enjoy *The Abyss of Comfort*.

## Stakeholder routing

| Stakeholder | Entry question | What to show | Misleading opening |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hobby creator | How can I enjoy continuing my project? | approachable causal exercise | publish/business pressure |
| Production creator | How can I structure and maintain continuity? | constraints, artifacts, release gates | unearned bestseller advice |
| Student | How can I reason through my own assignment? | guided structure and authorship | do-the-homework-for-you |
| Sci-fantasy reader | Why does this world matter to my life? | familiar human conflict and story | thousands of raw lore terms |
| Supporter | Why is continuation valuable? | evidence, deliverables and honest release status | imaginary demand numbers |
| Engineer/collaborator | What problem does this interface solve? | technical contract tied to reader need | buzzword stack showcase |

## Distinct transmission jobs
- **Marketing:** “Why should I look?” — relevance and qualified discovery.
- **Communication:** “What is this?” — shared understanding.
- **Sales:** “Why does this fit my specific need and terms?” — diagnosis and voluntary value matching.
- **Experience:** “Was the promised value delivered?” — inspectable outcomes and quality.
- **Retention/advocacy:** “Would I voluntarily return or recommend?” — repeated experience.

These may interact but are not one guaranteed funnel. Content reach does not prove buyers. Listening, audience-frame comprehension and micro→macro funneling matter as much as internal knowledge.

## Claim-level ladder
OBSERVATION (“I noticed”) → METHOD (“I use”) → DOCUMENTED EVIDENCE → EXTERNAL ADOPTION → VALIDATED METHODOLOGY. Stay in the level justified by evidence. Do not posture as a publisher, influencer-growth authority, coach for clinical burnout, or commercially established franchise.

## Reusable content atom
~~~text
1 problem / question
+ 1 causal model
+ 1 specific example
+ 1 honest limitation
+ 1 relevant discussion prompt
= 1 reusable intellectual asset
~~~

Adapt that asset for Reddit discussion, professional LinkedIn explanation, visual Instagram carousel, brief Threads reflection and deeper Discord dialogue. Do **not** copy/paste spam or violate community-specific promotion rules.

## Quality gate
Ask: WHO listens? WHAT concern is real? WHAT resolution fits? WHICH causal mechanism can be demonstrated? WHICH artifact proves it? WHICH claim is still a hypothesis? WHAT is the natural next depth?
