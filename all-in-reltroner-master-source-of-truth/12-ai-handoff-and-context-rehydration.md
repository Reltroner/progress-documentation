> Intended user: the next AI, architect, developer, reviewer or collaborator joining the project.  
> Snapshot: 2026-10-12 Asia/Jakarta. **Never treat this snapshot as present-day live verification.**

## First 90 seconds — correct entry order
1. Open [README](./README.md) and [00 Authority/Evidence](./00-authority-evidence-and-scope.md).
2. Determine the user request's **domain**: Studio/brand, narrative canon, LMS engineering, mentorship/business, community, or financial/tool spending.
3. Read the topic archive file. Follow its authoritative source pointers.
4. If LMS is involved, **read [lms/README.md](../lms/README.md) first and latest [ledger](../lms/engineering-end-to-end-progress-ledger.md)**; read the frozen physical/logical contracts before proposing changes.
5. Identify current SHA, gate, owner authorization and outstanding unknowns before touching infrastructure or declaring PASS.

## Compact stable context
- Brand: **Systemic Structural Creative Design × Software Engineering × Self Improvement** as a synergistic capability system, not three random startups.
- Apex value: Creative Compound Machine and 2026+-anxiety-driven advanced Asthortera civilizational simulation.
- Creator promise is a method for compounding creative possibilities; audience promise is meaningful science-fantasy experiences. Burnout prevention, publishing success, fan engagement uplift and revenue are unproven.

- Technology (LMS, website, Keycloak, APIs, automation) is **medium/access infrastructure**, not the core product for sale.
- Studio Master v2.3 ratified Phase 1 product foundation then Phase 2 community focus. **Phase 1 is not certified complete in this archive**. No active multi-channel branding campaign or mentorship launch is authorized solely by this document.
- LMS is **microservices, not modular monolith**, with six frozen runtime services: Gateway, Learning, Mentorship, Knowledge, Assistant, Audit. Existing HRM engineering discipline can be used, not HRM application topology.
- Identity canonical issuer/realm and client names come from frozen LMS contract. Do not invent realm/client/session migrations just to implement a preferred UX.
- Learning material source control is canonical; Knowledge indexing and AI are derived. Initial guest reading/public search is static-first, assistant API authenticated-only.
- Paid mentorship 1:1, Google Meet/Zoom, user-preferred WhatsApp/Discord/email reminders, n8n workflows and AI mentor agent are business/technical **candidates**, not necessarily deployed functionality.
- Source historical and technical checkpoints can be superseded by later evidence. Explicitly reconcile.

## What has been ratified vs not
**Ratified:** apex positioning; phase sequencing; non-copying of other influencers' marketing frameworks; user-friendly Reltroner-owned UX as intention; Phase 2 maintenance-first policy; truthful evidence ladder. **Frozen technical:** actual LMS six-service and infrastructure contracts. **Open:** commercial offer price/need; launch time; AI agents, LLM Wiki, pgvector, guest chatbot; exact operational implementation; current production acceptance.

## AI working style / safe collaboration contract
The user prefers explicit deterministic explanation, clear distinctions between read-only discovery and mutation, no invented result, minimal spending, scope freezes, CLI/SSH/GUI role clarity, exact SHA/branch and acceptance gates, isolated changes, no deployment without authorization.

When a long process is requested, provide:
- known facts with bounded evidence;
- uncertainty/gaps and what needs discovery;
- proposed minimal scope;
- owner approval gate if mutation is consequential;
- implementation/work order and exact acceptance evidence;
- final PASS/PARTIAL/FAILED with links/SHA rather than optimistic summaries.

Never claim successful production, payment, scheduling, source merge or tool integration before seeing the relevant result. Do not promise autonomous background operations that were not actually scheduled.

## Typical conflict resolution
**Question:** “Can we move LMS to a separate public realm and BFF cookies?”  
**Answer:** not implicitly. Read frozen OIDC clients/realm; define threat model, migration/cutover and ADR first.

**Question:** “Could Hermes replace the LMS Assistant/Search Service?”  
**Answer:** external agent may be candidate, but frozen business authority, protected retrieval and six-service boundaries are not erased.

**Question:** “Should we publish unfinished chapters to build an audience?”  
**Answer:** no such requirement. Preserve rewrite freedom before canonical freeze; publish non-spoiler method and process when strategic Phase 2 actually activates.

**Question:** “Start Discord + Reddit + LinkedIn + Instagram + Threads today?”  
**Answer:** no. Phase 1 product DoD + landing + owner exit precedes active Phase 2 campaign; choose limited channels based on cost/feedback.

**Question:** “Does README/CI prove LMS is production ready?”  
**Answer:** CI supports a bounded engineering gate. Production requires live runtime/security/ops tests and accepted owner scope.
